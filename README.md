# GambaOS

### <b>[!] Disclaimer: GambaOS is NOT na operating system! [!]</b>

GambaOS is a virtual environment with languages specifically designed for GambaOS.<br>
PyroLang, for example, is the programming language designed for the GambaOS web and backend.

I am currently working on turning GambaOS into a game where you can write your own programs.<br>
These programs can do anything from web development to your own game.

## The Internet
The internet of GambaOS is 100% local.<br>
This means no-one outside of your laptop can view your data, not even us!

### The Bank
GambaOS has a bank to keep track of how much money you have.<br>
The bank also handles transfers of money.

### The Market
At the market you can purchase useful things.<br>
The market uses the bank as transferring of money and is reliable.

### The Dark Market
At the dark market you can purchase very useful things.<br>
These things are mostly very cheap, but they can be decoys.<br>
This means that when you purchase it the data of your GambaOS instance can get to simulated hackers.<br>
Decoys can also just not give you anything in return for your money.

### The Cloud
You can save your files to the InkCloud, this is currently the only communication system between instances.<br>
The InkCloud works by saving the chosen files to GambaOS's APPDATA folder.

## Applications
In applications you can do all sorts of things that range from editing files to exploring the web.<br>

### Text Editor
In the text editor you can edit your scripts or texts.

### Calculator
This is just a normal calculator.

### File Explorer
In the file explorer you can see your directories and open files in the text editor.

### Terminal
In the terminal you can execute commands.<br>

### Web Explorer
In the web explorer you can go to different websites.<br>
